# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

"Mahjong Score Calculator" (hereinafter referred to as "the App") respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

**The App does not collect any personal information from users.**

All data in the App (user settings, display state, etc.) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

To improve user experience, the App stores the following information in local storage (AsyncStorage) on your device:

- App settings (default keyboard, display options, etc.)
- Display state of the Yaku (hand types) list

This information is used solely for the operation of the App and is never transmitted from your device to any external destination.

## 3. Disclosure to Third Parties

The App does not share user information with any third party.
The App does not use any third-party services such as advertising, analytics, or crash reporting.

## 4. Network Communication

The App does not perform any internet communication.
Because all operations are completed locally on your device, no network permission is required.

## 5. Data Deletion

When you uninstall the App, all data stored on your device is automatically deleted.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to any specific age group. Because the App does not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as necessary. If significant changes are made, we will notify users through an App update or via this page.
